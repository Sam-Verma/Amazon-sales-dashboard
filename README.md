# 📊 Amazon Sales Dashboard

## Overview

The **Amazon Sales Dashboard** is a Power BI project designed to analyze and visualize Amazon sales data. This interactive dashboard provides valuable insights into sales performance, customer behavior, product trends, and regional performance, enabling data-driven business decisions.

The report helps stakeholders monitor key metrics, identify growth opportunities, and optimize sales strategies through intuitive visualizations and comprehensive analytics.

---

## 🚀 Features

### 📈 Sales Trends Analysis

* Track monthly and yearly sales performance.
* Identify seasonal patterns and revenue growth trends.
* Monitor sales fluctuations over time.

### 🛍️ Product Performance Analysis

* Analyze top-performing and underperforming products.
* Evaluate product-wise sales contribution.
* Identify best-selling categories and items.

### 🌍 Geographical Insights

* Visualize sales distribution across regions and countries.
* Compare regional performance metrics.
* Discover high-performing markets and growth opportunities.

### 👥 Customer Segmentation

* Understand customer demographics and purchasing behavior.
* Analyze customer groups based on sales patterns.
* Support targeted marketing and customer retention strategies.

### 📊 KPI Dashboard

Monitor critical business metrics, including:

* Total Sales
* Total Orders
* Average Order Value (AOV)
* Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
* Revenue Growth Rate
* Product Performance Metrics
